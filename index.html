Beside it rests a small Garnet — his birthstone — a token of his protection and devotion to her.

A cherished tradition

The Tea Ceremony

Of all the wedding traditions we chose to forgo, this is one we decided to keep.

✦   ✦   ✦

The tea ceremony is our way of thanking our parents and elders who raised us, shaped us, and sacrificed so that we can be who we are today. It carries our gratitude, love, and respect.

It is also the moment we welcome each other's families as our own, and express our deepest gratitude to our loved ones, including those who are no longer with us. Having all of you here means everything to us.

Two families, one cup of tea, and a lifetime of gratitude.

Dress Code

Formal Attire

Smart formal is encouraged — suits and ties for the gentlemen, cocktail dresses, midi dresses, or formal gowns for the ladies. When in doubt: if you'd wear it to a wedding, you're on the right track!

👠

Shoes

The event is held indoors at a hotel restaurant, so wear whatever makes you look and feel great. Heels, dress shoes, or smart flats all work perfectly.
