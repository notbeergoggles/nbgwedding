Our Story

We were primary school classmates who never quite crossed paths, just two kids sharing the same classroom without a single conversation.


Fast forward to the pandemic, and a rhythm game called Muse Dash would change everything. Scrolling through Twitch one quiet evening, he stumbled upon a familiar face mid-stream and typed the first "hi" that started it all.


He became her most loyal viewer, tuning in night after night, until they discovered they had been living just five minutes apart the whole time. It was she who made the first bold move, suggesting supper at a nearby kopitiam after one of her streams.


Five years, countless late-night meals, a shared love of MilkMochaBear, and one unforgettable proposal in Kyoto later, here we are, ready to begin the next adventure together.

Dress Code

👔

Formal Attire

Dress to impress! Smart formal attire is encouraged — suits and ties for the gentlemen, and cocktail dresses, midi dresses, or formal gowns for the ladies. Think wedding-ready and you're on the right track.

🎨

Color Palette

Our solemnisation palette is soft and romantic — think pinks, blues, and purples. Feel free to complement the theme, though any formal color is warmly welcome. Please avoid white and icy blue, as those are reserved for the couple!

👠

Shoes

Our solemnisation is set in a rooftop garden, so please be mindful of your footwear. We'd recommend block heels or wedges over stilettos, and sturdy dress shoes for the gentlemen. Comfort matters — you'll want to enjoy the celebration!
